# Published trajectory families

Accepted on 2026-11-12: simulate the same 36 transverse neighbours for all eleven rho values with four methods on Modal JAX, preserve the original 48 particles and publish a separate grouped viewer.

Published and verified: [new viewer](https://gc2d-poincare-families.pages.dev/) and [this deployment](https://f3307e06.gc2d-poincare-families.pages.dev/). All 44 parameter combinations and 1584 new trajectories passed archive audits. The combined site has 84 particles, 5001 returns per trajectory and 3696 validated Qx values. All 2112 original metric values and all original plotted samples are unchanged. Eleven hashed assets load on demand; the largest is 18.36 MiB. The historical radial Hamiltonian, source field, normalization and fixed step are preserved.

The initial Newton solve and its damped 80-iteration retry failed for BM4Implicit at rho=0.05; both results were excluded. A diagnostic found an original-control failure at cycle 1351.54, with residual 7.07824e-8 versus tolerance 1.1e-11. The 36 independent-particle executions then all completed using the original 40-iteration cap, tolerances and 50 steps/cycle. Their first twenty cycles differ from the joint short run by at most 4.06306e-9 m. Each archive was validated before merging; all call IDs and the changed grouping are recorded and identified in the viewer. BM4Midpoint at rho=0.15 also completed after a confirmed timeout and an unchanged-parameter retry.

Seed geometry checks give a maximum normal-angle disagreement of 0.794583 degrees and offsets from 0.003710916 to 0.250334287 mm. All twelve initial short Modal benchmarks passed, as did two short damping comparisons and two timeout-diagnostic runs. One-cycle 50/100-step refinement gives maximum endpoint differences of 5.96e-10 m for BM4, 2.39e-8 m for RK4 and 2.12e-8 m for GL4; the largest relative change in absolute-velocity integral is 0.001593. These checks do not certify long-time convergence or periodicity, and separately stopped Newton solves can accumulate different rounding errors.

Real-data browser checks passed for 84 individual controls, nine five-member families and 39 other originals, isolation, partial/group selection, family zoom, selection across rho=0.30/0.50/0.05 and interval-independent Qx. No JavaScript errors were observed. The public default shows particle 1 as 0.31200 and particle 49 as 0.32149 in all four methods. Fourteen HTTP checks passed; the served HTML, metrics, provenance and rho=0.30/0.05 assets match local hashes. An earlier unlabeled synthetic UI fixture had shown fictitious repeated Qx=10.500; its tab/server were closed and it was never published.

Strict mypy passes for 317 source files. Seven focused tests, both smoke examples, JavaScript syntax, the final sdist/wheel build and SDD checks pass. The full suite ran 770 tests in 929.686 seconds with three skips, five initial failures and two errors. Retired bytecode-only namespaces caused the failures and were preserved outside the source tree; a template delimiter regression caused the errors and was fixed. Every affected check passed in the six-test rerun. The full suite was not repeated after those corrections.

The measured workspace usage increase was approximately USD 1.6228, below the USD 6 study limit, including failed attempts and recovery. Receipts, audits, comparisons, browser checks, copied validation logs, billing snapshots and `cloudflare_pages/deployment.json` are retained under the ignored `notebooks/developements/poincare_section/parallel_poincare_families/` directory. The original website and source HDF5 remain unchanged. No implementation or publication work remains pending.
