# Proposed behavior

IntegrationMethod retains field functions, numerical metadata, run status and copy count. Remove particle_count, physical_size, layout, initial_state, jacobian_structure, progress, requires_planar, observe and capture_quadrature from the base. InitialConditions remains a temporary preparation input; execution owns initial snapshots and progress. Supplied Jacobians use independent particle blocks (N,d,d); finite differences remain dense. Keep constructor validation, one new_run entry and explicit family preparation. Preserve isolated JAX solver binding.

Remove adaptive methods from the simulation facade and runtime. Standalone high-accuracy study references call SciPy directly. EnergyObserver records H and analytic positive partial_t H at accepted endpoints and requested physical samples, plus energy differences. It never estimates the derivative by finite differences, integrates kappa, or consumes method stages. GC uses the already normalized Hamiltonian potential; Fourier time differentiation uses dt=1. Variation of time-dependent H is not a conservation error. Specialized historical extended-energy studies retain explicitly separate diagnostics and their stage mathematics; historical archives are never relabeled.
