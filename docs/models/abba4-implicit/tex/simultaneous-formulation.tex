\section{Purpose and scope}

The \texttt{simultaneous\_state\_multiplier} option of
\texttt{ABBA4Implicit} computes the same ideal physical map as the
\texttt{reduced\_multiplier} option, but it retains the duplicated output state
as part of each nonlinear solve.  One fourth-order step contains three
independent projected ABBA substeps.  Consequently, for one guiding-centre
particle, it solves three $6\times6$ nonlinear systems rather than three
$2\times2$ reduced Schur-complement systems.

This note describes the physical-state branch.  The
\texttt{shared\_time} branch uses the same physical projected solves and adds a
triangular update of the accepted conjugate momentum.  The
\texttt{fully\_extended} branch duplicates the complete autonomous state and
therefore uses different dimensions.

The two physical formulations agree whenever each projection root is locally
unique and both nonlinear algorithms converge to that root.  Finite stopping
tolerances and floating-point linear solves can produce round-off-level
differences.

\section{Fourth-order signed composition}

Define the triple-jump coefficients
\[
 \gamma=\frac{1}{2-2^{1/3}},
 \qquad
 \delta=-\frac{2^{1/3}}{2-2^{1/3}},
 \qquad
 (c_1,c_2,c_3)=(\gamma,\delta,\gamma).
\]
They satisfy
\[
 c_1+c_2+c_3=1,
 \qquad
 c_1^3+c_2^3+c_3^3=0.
\]
For an outer step $(t_n,z_n)\mapsto(t_n+h,z_{n+1})$, set
\[
 \tau_1=t_n,
 \qquad
 \tau_2=t_n+c_1h,
 \qquad
 \tau_3=t_n+(c_1+c_2)h,
\]
and
\[
 \ell_j=c_jh,
 \qquad
 \tau_j^+=\tau_j+\ell_j.
\]
The chronological physical updates are
\[
 z^{[0]}=z_n,
 \qquad
 z^{[j]}=\PsiTwo_{\ell_j,\tau_j}(z^{[j-1]}),
 \quad j=1,2,3,
 \qquad
 z_{n+1}=z^{[3]}.
\]
The middle duration $\ell_2=\delta h$ is negative.  Its endpoint precedes its
start, but the same endpoint-time formulas remain valid without a special
backward branch.

\section{Duplicated state and one signed ABBA map}

The physical state $z=(X,Y)\in\R^2$ satisfies
\[
 \dot z=f(z,t),
 \qquad
 f(z,t)=J_0\nabla_z\phi(z,t),
 \qquad
 J_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its spatial Jacobian is
\[
 W(z,t)=D_zf(z,t)=J_0\nabla_z^2\phi(z,t).
\]
Introduce the duplicated state and the diagonal and correction maps
\[
 \Y=(u,v)\in\R^4,
 \qquad
 G=(\Id_2\;-\Id_2),
 \qquad
 N=G^T=\begin{pmatrix}\Id_2\\-\Id_2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 E=\begin{pmatrix}\Id_2\\\Id_2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus $Ez=(z,z)$ lies on
\(
 \M=\{(u,v):u=v\}
\), $N\mu=(\mu,-\mu)$ separates the copies, and
\[
 GE=0,
 \qquad
 GN=2\Id_2.
\]

For substep $j$, let $s_j=\ell_j/2$.  Starting from
$(u_j^{(0)},v_j^{(0)})$, the signed endpoint-time ABBA map is
\begin{align*}
 u_j^{(1)}&=u_j^{(0)}+s_j f(v_j^{(0)},\tau_j),\\
 v_j^{(1)}&=v_j^{(0)}+s_j f(u_j^{(1)},\tau_j),\\
 v_j^{(2)}&=v_j^{(1)}+s_j f(u_j^{(1)},\tau_j^+),\\
 u_j^{(2)}&=u_j^{(1)}+s_j f(v_j^{(2)},\tau_j^+).
\end{align*}
We denote the resulting duplicated map by
\[
 \PhiAB_{\ell_j,\tau_j}(u_j^{(0)},v_j^{(0)})
 =(u_j^{(2)},v_j^{(2)}).
\]

At its four stage points define
\[
 W_{j,1}=W(v_j^{(0)},\tau_j),
 \quad
 W_{j,2}=W(u_j^{(1)},\tau_j),
 \quad
 W_{j,3}=W(u_j^{(1)},\tau_j^+),
 \quad
 W_{j,4}=W(v_j^{(2)},\tau_j^+),
\]
and $S_j=W_{j,2}+W_{j,3}$.  The exact duplicated-space Jacobian is
\[
 J_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 \Id_2+s_j^2W_{j,4}S_j
 &s_j(W_{j,1}+W_{j,4})+s_j^3W_{j,4}S_jW_{j,1}\\[1mm]
 s_jS_j
 &\Id_2+s_j^2S_jW_{j,1}
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The factor order is essential because the stage Jacobians do not commute in
general.

\section{Simultaneous equations for substep \texorpdfstring{$j$}{j}}

Given $z^{[j-1]}$, pre-correct the diagonal input with an independent
multiplier $\mu_j$:
\[
 \widehat{\Y}_j(\mu_j)
 =Ez^{[j-1]}+N\mu_j
 =(z^{[j-1]}+\mu_j,z^{[j-1]}-\mu_j).
\]
The simultaneous unknown is
\[
 (\Y_j^+,\mu_j)\in\R^4\times\R^2,
 \qquad
 \Y_j^+=(y_{u,j},y_{v,j}).
\]
It is determined by
\[
 d_j(\Y_j^+,\mu_j)
 :=\Y_j^+-N\mu_j
 -\PhiAB_{\ell_j,\tau_j}
   \bigl(Ez^{[j-1]}+N\mu_j\bigr)=0,
\]
\[
 g_j(\Y_j^+):=G\Y_j^+=0.
\]
In component blocks, the residual is
\[
 d_{u,j}=y_{u,j}-\mu_j-u_j^{(2)}(\mu_j),
 \qquad
 d_{v,j}=y_{v,j}+\mu_j-v_j^{(2)}(\mu_j),
 \qquad
 g_j=y_{u,j}-y_{v,j}.
\]
At convergence the accepted physical state is represented neutrally by
\[
 z^{[j]}=\frac{y_{u,j}+y_{v,j}}{2}.
\]
The mean removes the antisymmetric component left by finite stopping tolerance;
at the exact root both output copies are identical.

\section{Exact simultaneous Newton system}

Because $G$ and $N$ are constant,
\[
 D_{\Y_j^+}d_j=\Id_4,
 \qquad
 D_{\mu_j}d_j
 =-\bigl(\Id_4+J_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}\bigr)N,
\]
\[
 D_{\Y_j^+}g_j=G,
 \qquad
 D_{\mu_j}g_j=0.
\]
Each Newton correction therefore solves
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \Id_4&-\bigl(\Id_4+J_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}\bigr)N\\[1mm]
  G&0
 \end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}
  \Delta\Y_j^+\\
  \Delta\mu_j
 \end{pmatrix}
 =-
 \begin{pmatrix}
  d_j\\g_j
 \end{pmatrix}.
 }
 \tag{1}
\]
The update adds $(\Delta\Y_j^+,\Delta\mu_j)$ to the current iterate.  No
matrix inverse is formed: the implementation solves one dense $6\times6$
system per independent particle.

The initial iterate is
\[
 \mu_j^{(0)}=0,
 \qquad
 \Y_j^{+,(0)}
 =\PhiAB_{\ell_j,\tau_j}(Ez^{[j-1]}).
\]
It makes $d_j=0$ initially, while the generally nonzero copy difference $g_j$
drives the first correction.

For
\[
 \sigma_j=\max\bigl(1,\norm{z^{[j-1]}}_\infty\bigr),
\]
the solve stops when
\[
 \max\bigl(\norm{d_j}_\infty,\norm{g_j}_\infty\bigr)
 \leq \mathrm{atol}+\mathrm{rtol}\,\sigma_j.
\]
Each of the three substeps has its own scale, iteration count, and stopping
decision.

\section{Schur complement and reduced-formulation equivalence}

Eliminating $\Delta\Y_j^+$ from (1) produces
\[
 K_j
 =G\bigl(\Id_4+J_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}\bigr)G^T
 =2\Id_2+GJ_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}G^T.
\]
The reduced residual is
\[
 R_j(\mu_j)
 =G\PhiAB_{\ell_j,\tau_j}
   \bigl(Ez^{[j-1]}+G^T\mu_j\bigr)+2\mu_j,
\]
and $K_j=D_{\mu_j}R_j$.  Moreover,
\[
 Gd_j=g_j-R_j(\mu_j).
\]
Substitution into the second block row of (1) gives
\[
 K_j\Delta\mu_j=-R_j(\mu_j),
\]
which is exactly the reduced Newton correction.  With equal initial
multipliers, the two formulations therefore generate the same multiplier
iterates in exact arithmetic.  The simultaneous branch additionally updates
the duplicated output workspace.

This equivalence is local to each signed substep.  ABBA4 still requires three
different roots because $(z^{[j-1]},\tau_j,\ell_j)$ changes with $j$.

\section{Broyden and multiple-particle layout}

Selecting \texttt{nonlinear\_solver="broyden"} preserves the simultaneous
residual $(d_j,g_j)$ but replaces the exact Newton matrix by a good rank-one
secant update.  The initial approximation has the same block structure as (1)
with the unprojected Jacobian approximated by the identity.  Broyden changes
the root-finding algorithm, not the defining projected map.

For $N_p$ independent guiding-centre particles, accepted physical states use
component-major storage,
\[
 (X_1,\ldots,X_{N_p},Y_1,\ldots,Y_{N_p}).
\]
Newton reshapes this storage into $N_p$ independent $6\times6$ blocks and packs
the corrections back into component-major order.  Broyden instead treats the
complete simultaneous vector as one system of dimension $6N_p$.

\section{Symmetry and symplecticity of the complete map}

At an exact root, every substep satisfies
\[
 \Y_j^+-N\mu_j
 =\PhiAB_{\ell_j,\tau_j}
  \bigl(Ez^{[j-1]}+N\mu_j\bigr),
 \qquad
 \Y_j^+\in\M.
\]
The endpoint-time ABBA map is self-adjoint under a signed-step reversal.  The
symmetric projection then gives
\[
 \PsiTwo_{-\ell_j,\tau_j^+}
 \circ\PsiTwo_{\ell_j,\tau_j}=\Id.
\]
Because $(\gamma,\delta,\gamma)$ is palindromic, the complete composition obeys
\[
 \PsiFour_{-h,t_n+h}\circ\PsiFour_{h,t_n}=\Id.
\]

Each elementary ABBA shear preserves the duplicated form
\[
 \Om=\begin{pmatrix}0&J_0\\J_0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hairer's symmetric projection restricts that preservation to the physical
diagonal, so the ideal physical Jacobian $J_j$ of each signed substep satisfies
\[
 J_j^TJ_0J_j=J_0.
\]
The outer Jacobian is $J_3J_2J_1$ and is therefore symplectic as well.  In
finite arithmetic, both reversibility and symplecticity include the defects of
all three nonlinear solves.

\section{Implementation correspondence}

The production path mirrors the derivation:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item \texttt{order4\_implicit.py} creates the three signed durations and
       passes the accepted physical output of one solve into the next;
 \item \texttt{\_projection\_simultaneous.py} evaluates $(d_j,g_j)$ and
       assembles the particle-wise matrix (1);
 \item \texttt{\_projection\_common.py} evaluates the endpoint-time stages and
       $J_{\mathrm{ABBA},j}$;
 \item each accepted outer step stores three ordered substep observations and
       three columns of nonlinear diagnostics;
 \item aggregate diagnostics sum iteration and residual-evaluation counts,
       retain the worst normalized residual, and record the maximum multiplier
       norm.
\end{enumerate}

The three simultaneous systems are solver workspaces.  They are not trajectory
states and do not introduce a fourth composition stage or a new public method.
