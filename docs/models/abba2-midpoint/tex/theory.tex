\section{Definition}

For $\dot z=f(t,z)$, $z\in\R^m$, duplicate the accepted state on the diagonal,
$u_0=v_0=z_n$, and write $s=h/2$.  The endpoint-time ABBA stages are
\begin{align}
u_1&=u_0+s f(t_n,v_0),&
v_1&=v_0+s f(t_n,u_1),\\
v_f&=v_1+s f(t_n+h,u_1),&
u_f&=u_1+s f(t_n+h,v_f).
\end{align}
The physical output is the midpoint of the copies,
\[
z_{n+1}=\frac{u_f+v_f}{2}.
\]
``Midpoint'' therefore refers to this arithmetic projection; the method is not
the implicit-midpoint Runge--Kutta rule.

\section{State-extension configurations}

The model has no multiplier, residual, Newton iteration, or Broyden update.
Its only numerical configuration axis is \texttt{state\_extension}:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Value & accepted internal state & duplicated base state\\
\midrule
\texttt{physical} & $z\in\R^{2N}$ & $(u,v)\in\R^{4N}$\\
\texttt{shared\_time} & $(z,t,\kappa)\in\R^4$ & $(u,v,t,k)\in\R^6$\\
\texttt{fully\_extended} & $Z=(z,t,k)\in\R^4$ & $(Z_1,Z_2)\in\R^8$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The last two rows currently apply to one guiding-centre particle.  Shared time
duplicates only the physical coordinates and stores $\kappa=k/2$; the
triangular momentum update does not feed back into the physical trajectory.
Fully extended mode duplicates all of $Z$ and may therefore define a different
physical map.

\section{Symmetry, order, and geometry}

The four shears form an explicit self-adjoint map on the duplicated phase
space when the signed step reverses together with its endpoint times.  Starting
from the diagonal and taking the arithmetic mean yields a consistent symmetric
method with designed global order two for sufficiently smooth dynamics.

The duplicated ABBA map is symplectic for its split Hamiltonian.  Arithmetic
projection, however, is not Hairer's symmetric projection and does not in
general make the closed physical map symplectic.  The copy-separation quantity
\[
d_n=\lVert u_f-v_f\rVert_\infty
\]
is therefore retained as a diagnostic.  It is neither an error estimator nor
a convergence residual.

\section{Work and time dependence}

One step performs four vector-field evaluations.  The evaluations at $t_n$
and $t_n+h$ are part of the non-autonomous definition.  The common fixed-grid
runner chooses a uniform main grid independently of requested output times;
off-grid output uses shadow steps that do not alter the accepted main
trajectory or its diagnostics.

\section{Implementation correspondence}

The public class is implemented in
\texttt{src/simulation/methods/abba/order2\_midpoint.py}.  It reuses the common
endpoint-time stages from \texttt{abba/\_core.py}; shared-time and fully
extended kernels are selected by the same public class.  The companion
architecture document under \texttt{../simulation/} records the complete
runtime and observation contract.
